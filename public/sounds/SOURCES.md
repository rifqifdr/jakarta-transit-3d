No KRL recording is openly licensed. The announcements KAI Commuter plays on its
trains are its own, and recordings of them online are their uploaders' — none of
it is ours to ship. So the train sound is a generic EMU interior (KRL runs
ex-Japanese EMUs, so it is the right kind of train), the announcer is the
device's own Indonesian text-to-speech, and the chime before it is synthesised
in `src/ride-sound.js`, not a copy of KRL's.

CC0 asks for no credit; it is given anyway in *Sumber data*.
